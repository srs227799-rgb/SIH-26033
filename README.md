## 📖 About the Project

FarmLink is a proposed digital marketplace designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, supermarkets, hotels, and bulk buyers.

In the traditional agricultural supply chain, multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase prices for consumers.

FarmLink aims to solve this problem by creating a direct digital connection between farmers and buyers.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence (AI), smart logistics, and demand forecasting to improve agricultural product distribution.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Increase farmers' income through direct selling.
- Reduce unnecessary intermediaries.
- Provide affordable and fresh agricultural products to consumers.
- Improve transportation and delivery efficiency.
- Support rural farmers through digital technology.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Direct Marketplace
Farmers can upload their agricultural products, available quantities, and expected prices. Buyers can directly purchase products from them.

### 2. AI-Based Price Prediction
AI suggests suitable product prices based on market trends, demand, and availability.

### 3. Smart Logistics
Connects farmers with nearby transport and delivery services to simplify product transportation.

### 4. Demand Forecasting
Machine Learning predicts future demand for vegetables and agricultural products to reduce overproduction and food wastage.

### 5. Bulk Buying
Hotels, supermarkets, restaurants, and retailers can purchase agricultural products directly from farmers in large quantities.

### 6. Live Order Tracking
Buyers can track their orders from the farm to the delivery destination.

### 7. Regional Language Support
Provides Tamil and other regional language options to make the platform accessible to rural farmers.

## 🔄 How FarmLink Works

1. **Farmer Registration:** Farmers register on the FarmLink platform.
2. **Product Upload:** Farmers upload product names, quantities, and prices.
3. **AI Analysis:** The system analyses market demand and suggests suitable prices.
4. **Product Discovery:** Consumers and bulk buyers browse available products.
5. **Order Placement:** Buyers place orders directly with farmers.
6. **Logistics Connection:** The platform connects farmers with nearby transport providers.
7. **Product Delivery:** Agricultural products are delivered to buyers.
8. **Order Tracking:** Buyers can track their orders until delivery.
