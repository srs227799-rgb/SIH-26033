## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution 

Our proposed solution is to develop a digital platform called FarmLink, which directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers and bulk buyers.

The platform removes unnecessary intermediaries and helps farmers sell their products at fair prices.

Main features:
1. Direct Marketplace – Farmers can upload their products, quantity and expected price.
2. AI Price Prediction – AI suggests a fair selling price based on demand and market trends.
3. Smart Logistics – Connects farmers with nearby transport services.
4. Demand Forecasting – Predicts which vegetables and crops will have higher demand.
5. Bulk Buying – Hotels, supermarkets and shops can purchase products directly from farmers.
6. Live Tracking – Buyers can track their orders from farm to destination.
7. Local Language Support – Farmers can use the application in Tamil and other regional languages.
How it solves the problem:

Instead of selling products through multiple middlemen, farmers can directly connect with buyers. This reduces unnecessary costs, increases farmers' earnings and provides affordable products to consumers.

## Feasibility and Viability

Feasibility:

FarmLink can be developed using existing web technologies, AI tools and mapping services. It can initially be tested in a small agricultural area and expanded to other regions.

Potential challenges:
1. Some farmers may not know how to use digital applications.
2. Transportation costs may be high for distant locations.
3. Product prices may change frequently.
4. Some farmers may not have reliable internet access.
Strategies to overcome challenges:
1. Provide a simple Tamil and regional-language interface.
2. Partner with local transport providers.
3. Update market prices regularly using available market data.
4. Provide offline support and SMS notifications.
